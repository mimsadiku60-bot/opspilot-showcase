THE PROBLEM

The message is only
the start of the work.

Someone still needs to understand the request, route it, track the task and review the reply. OpsPilot brings those steps into one case so the next person does not have to reconstruct the context.

A 60-SECOND WALKTHROUGH

One case.
Four things to notice.

A presentation script, not a recorded video. Run the fictional damaged-order scenario in the live demo before timing the walkthrough.

  1. 00–15s

    Read the request

    Open AI understanding. Show order #1048, the five affected units and the requested Friday deadline.

    “The request and the known facts are together. Anything unknown stays unknown.”

  2. 15–30s

    Find the next step

    Open Internal task. Show the linked review task, Customer Support routing and priority.

    “The team can see the work to follow up without rebuilding the context. Routing is not fulfilment.”

  3. 30–45s

    Review before acting

    Open Draft & approval. Inspect or edit the draft as the reviewer, then approve the fictional response.

    “A person controls the wording and the decision. Nothing is sent outside the demo.”

  4. 45–60s

    Check what happened

    Inspect Execution trace or Audit trail. The completed workflow records a simulated response. The operational task still needs follow-up.

    “There is a record of the decision and a clear next step—not a claim of external delivery.”

SCOPE, WITHOUT THE HYPE

A working demonstration.
Clear implementation limits.

Demonstrated

What the portfolio shows

  • Structured requests, rule-based routing and persistent internal tasks.
  • Editable drafts and human approval, rejection or cancellation.
  • Execution and audit history, controlled failure and explicit retry.
  • A public interface backed by a versioned application and automated tests.
Separate implementation

What is not connected

  • No live Gmail, Outlook, Slack, ATS, CRM, n8n or Make account connections.
  • No real outbound messages, business actions or automatic hiring decisions.
  • No production authentication, tenant isolation or enterprise access controls.
  • No claimed customer deployments, revenue results, uptime guarantee or compliance certification.
Before any real client data: agree the scope, implement and test access controls, isolate the client environment, configure the required integrations and complete security and data-handling review. A demo setting alone does not supply these controls.

Public AI uses a deterministic demo/fallback provider; paid AI is disabled. “Demo Operator” and “Demo Approver” are attribution labels, not authenticated identities.
